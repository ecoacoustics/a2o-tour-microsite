## Why Your Contributions <em>Matter</em>

Located on Ngalia Warlpiri and Luritja Country, Newhaven protects a vast stretch of spinifex, mulga and desert oak country in central Australia. Many of its birds are nomads that arrive after rain or flowering and then move on, and your verifications help track when these species are present.

These records help researchers understand how arid-zone wildlife responds to the boom and bust of rainfall, fire and drought.
