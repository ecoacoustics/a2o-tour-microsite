## Why Your Contributions <em>Matter</em>

Located on palawa Country, truwana (Cape Barren Island) is cared for by the Truwana Rangers. Its forests, heath and wetlands support birds that are rarely surveyed in this remote part of Bass Strait, and your verifications help confirm which species are calling in each habitat.

These records support the rangers' work to monitor bird biodiversity on the island and to detect environmental change over time.
