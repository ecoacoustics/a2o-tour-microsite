## Why Your Contributions <em>Matter</em>

Located on the Country of the Koongurrukun, Mak Mak Marranunggu, Werat and Warray peoples, Litchfield's tropical savanna swings between a long dry season and a monsoonal wet. Your verifications help show how the bird community shifts as migrants arrive with the rains and flowering eucalypts draw in nectar feeders.

These records build a picture of savanna wildlife in a landscape shaped by fire and seasonal water.
